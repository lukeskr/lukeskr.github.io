I am a computer science researcher and software engineer specialising in mixed reality, interactive systems, computer vision, and computer graphics.

My work combines practical software engineering with applied research, with a particular focus on interactive instructional systems, real-time applications, and novel approaches to presenting and authoring procedural information. I independently conceive, design, and implement research systems, translating research ideas into functional prototypes and evaluating technical approaches through demonstrations and user studies.

I have contributed as a first-author and co-author to peer-reviewed publications at leading venues in mixed and augmented reality, and have collaborated with international, multidisciplinary research teams.

Research interests
======
- Augmented and virtual reality
- Interactive tutorial authoring and guidance
- Mixed reality instructional systems
- Real-time rendering and rendering systems
- Computer vision and camera tracking
- Human-computer interaction
